Notes:
  - Why: interior-point is affected by every constraint, even far-away
    ones, and landed in worse local minima with the convex hull (update 2).
    sqp is not. In a single-step test sqp found 0.0408 at flower1 'M'
    step 9 (interior-point: 0.066).
  - sqp only works with the convex hull (with all pixels it needs a
    107 GB matrix).
  - About 1 hour for all 4 images at 128x128 (update 2: ~1 h 45 min).
  - Comparison with update 2: not done yet.
